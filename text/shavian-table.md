# The Shavian Alphabet Reference Guide

The Shavian alphabet consists of four distinct character classes: Tall letters, mainly voiceless consonants; Their voiced counterparts, Deep letters; Short letters for vowels, liquids and nasals, and compounds for ease of writing.

---

* **𐑝** = *of*
* **𐑯** = *and*
* **𐑑** = *to*
